# Current visual design contract

Updated 2026-10-11. Working UI and product scope accepted by Brad.

Preserve the UCII dark navy, blue/green illumination, restrained status colors and responsive request/result workspace. Success must reflect a confirmed result; blocked/error feedback must remain distinct. Show sign-in only where required, inline Allow/Block confirmation, saved-list selection and compact Activity.

The working v36 interface is the current layout reference. Earlier large authority dashboards and multi-category navigation are concept history, not requirements to reintroduce.

Preserve the four existing master/derived assets under assets/: demo-ui PNG/SVG and thumbnail PNG/SVG. They remain useful branding/submission references, but must not imply purchases, deployment, live Alexa devices or unimplemented capabilities.

The product language remains: **Conversation is the interface. UCII is the control plane. Human authority always wins.**
